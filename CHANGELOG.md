# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Firefly Light: while the Glowing Jar is in the pocket, valuables within `Radius` metres (default 15) are highlighted for the owner every `RefreshSeconds` (default 1.5 s) and the jar wears 1.5% per second while at least one valuable is highlighted.
- Settings `Radius` (1 to 50) and `RefreshSeconds` (0.2 to 10).
- `Debug.DebugMode` (off by default): puts the Glowing Jar on the floor at the start of each level and writes detailed logs.
